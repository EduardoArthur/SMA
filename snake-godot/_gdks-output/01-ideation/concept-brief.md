## 🎯 Overview

### Logline
> **Snake: Same old rules, a very dramatic little snake.**

### Elevator Pitch
Snake is an arcade game where you play as a cute, expressive snake that eats fruit and grows longer in a single grid arena.

The core loop is: **steer → eat → grow → survive**, with a higher score as the reward.

What makes it unique: it plays by the classic rules, but everything reacts. The snake pulls faces, the fruit panics, and the screen bounces and pops, so every run feels lively and slightly chaotic.

Players will feel a laugh-out-loud panic when a near-miss makes the snake gasp and the whole screen wobbles.

Target audience: fans of short, replayable casual games such as the classic Nokia Snake, Google's Snake doodle and Slither.io-style arcade games.

### The Hook
**What makes this game unique:**
The rules stay classic and the presentation creates the chaos. The fun comes from character and game feel ("juice"), not from extra rules. This keeps the scope beginner-friendly while giving the game its own personality.

## 👤 Player Experience

### Core Fantasy
> "In Snake, you get to be **a greedy little snake who can't stop eating, even when it really should.**"

### Primary Emotions
The player should feel:
1. **Chaotic fun:** laughing at near-misses and silly reactions
2. **Rising tension:** less space as the snake gets longer
3. **"One more run":** a quick restart and a score to beat

### Defining Moments

| Moment | Description | Emotion |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| The Chomp | Eating a fruit: squash-and-stretch, a particle burst, a crunch sound and a satisfied face | Satisfaction |
| The Near-Miss | Turning just before hitting your own tail: the snake gasps, sweat drops and the camera nudges | Panic, then relief |
| The Dramatic Death | A collision: an exaggerated cartoon faint, a screen shake and a slow-motion freeze before the score screen | Laughter, "one more!" |

## 🔄 Gameplay Foundation

### Core Loop
```
[Steer] → [Eat] → [Grow] → [Higher score / survive longer]
```

### Primary Mechanics
1. **Grid movement:** The snake moves one cell per tick in four directions, and the player queues turns.
2. **Eat and grow:** Eating fruit adds a segment and points. A new fruit spawns on a free cell.
3. **Collision:** Hitting a wall or your own body ends the run.
4. **Speed ramp:** The tick rate increases slowly as the score rises.

### Player Progression
- **Short-term:** Eat the next fruit
- **Medium-term:** Beat the current run's milestones, such as length 10, 25 or 50
- **Long-term:** Beat your saved high score

## ⭐ Unique Selling Points

### Why This Game?
1. **A character, not a line:** The snake has emotions and reacts to everything.
2. **Heavy game feel:** Screen shake, squash-and-stretch, particles and punchy sounds on every action.
3. **Instantly familiar:** Anyone can play in 2 seconds, with no tutorial needed.

### Competitive Differentiation
| Comparable Game | Our Difference |

| Classic Nokia Snake | Same rules, but full of personality and juice |
| Google Snake (doodle) | More expressive character and more over-the-top reactions |
